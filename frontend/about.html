Slow coffee, on purpose

Why Kamil exists, and what we're trying to get right.

Named for something whole

Kamil means complete — and that's the standard we hold the cup to. Not fast, not average, not "good enough for a chain." Every pour is timed, every dessert is made that morning, and nobody behind the counter is rushing you out the door.

We roast in small batches, adjust the grind by the week's humidity, and steam milk to order rather than in advance. It's slower than it needs to be. That's the point.
