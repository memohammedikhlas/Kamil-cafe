Coffee, the long way round

Kamil opened as a small counter for people who wanted their coffee unhurried. Beans are roasted in small batches, milk is steamed to order, and nothing on the menu is built for a rush. Come for the coffee, stay till the candles burn low.

Every dessert is made in-house, the playlist changes with the hour, and the lighting is kept low enough that the espresso machine's brass fittings catch the glow.
